# The pipeline lane

Moved word for word from the Citadel scheme instructions (#760); the scheme text keeps a one-line
summary for the Taskmaster. The paths above are `tools/pipeline-files.txt`'s list: change both together.

**The pipeline lane** (Josh, 2026-11-12, on #580's review: "we can have only one working at a time, thats fair"). The land and deploy pipeline (`tools/land.sh` and its parts in `tools/land/`, `tools/deploy.sh`, `tools/deploy-queue.sh`, `tools/run-tests.sh`, `tools/citadel-wait`, `tools/citadel-heavy`, `tools/boot-check.ts`, `src/land.ts`, `.github/workflows/tests.yml`, the kit's land template and their tests) is changed by one minion at a time. The Taskmaster keeps the lane: a second pipeline minion ships only when the first is live, but for the two ways below. A fix for lands that are broken right now jumps the lane. Probe and stress runs on CI never use the lands' concurrency group. If your work touches these files and you don't hold the lane, build, test and review it as usual (Cara, 2026-11-21, "Build in parallel"), then call `hand_off_land` once (#2046; Josh, 2026-11-25, "Hand off once, ship when ready"): it is armed, not started. You join the end of the lane's queue by yourself, marked ready (the Taskmaster gets held mail and may reorder it), and the server ships it with no turn of yours when the lane passes to you or you are let in beside its holder. End your turn then and don't hand off again. Anyone writing to you, a turn of yours, a moved branch or a refused check rules the armed ship out: you are woken with why, and hand off again once it's ready.

**Two ways past a slow turn** (Josh, 2026-11-25, on #1985's card: "Skip a holder who isn't ready; Ship together if no shared files"). The server does both, for the ready minions in the lane's queue:
- Built, tested and reviewed while you wait? Call `hand_off_land`. It is armed for the lane and marks you ready.
- A holder that hands nothing off in 10 minutes is passed over for the first ready one, and waits first. A hand-off after being passed over is armed, and ships when the lane is yours again.
- While the holder's ship is in line, the next ready one may ship beside it, one at a time, when they change none of the same pipeline files: an armed one ships then with no turn, and one not yet handed off is told. The hand-off checks that again.
