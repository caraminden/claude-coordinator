# Overseers ask people when the spec is silent

Status: accepted, 2026-11-27. Built by #2204.

- **Cara, 2026-11-27,** after Build 10's Overseer "just did the whole thing without asking anything": asked on a card whether Overseers should ask during the build instead of saving their calls for the go card, she picked **"Ask when spec is silent"**.
- **Cara, 2026-11-26** (still holds): "Im not sure I care if the minions are asking the overseer questions?" Asks the Overseer answers itself stay on its board, off people's cards.

## The rule now

- The Overseer answers a member's ask itself when the build's spec, its decisions log or a person's earlier pick settles it, quoting the source on the board.
- When they are silent, or the answer would bend or reverse a pick, it asks the build's requester on a card with its pick marked. The asking member waits; others carry on. The answer goes into the decisions log, quoted and dated, and on the board.
- The go card lists the calls the Overseer made itself.
