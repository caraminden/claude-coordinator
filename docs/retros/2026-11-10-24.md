A two-week scheduled retro (#1795). Cara asked for it on 2026-11-24 and picked "Run a retro now". Its focus is
patterns that run across weeks, which the weekly ship-lessons pass can't see. The sources are the retro log, the
ship lessons, the four process digests (Nov 9, 16, 19 and 23) and the Nov 20 to 21 retro. Times are Pacific.

## Fixes

1. **Text fixes for a repeat didn't hold; mechanism fixes did.** `kit/schedules/ship-lessons.md`, step 3,
   now has the weekly pass read the lessons already taken. A theme still coming back after a take gets a
   tool, test, hook or gate change, or a card for a person, and no more text.
   - Refused stops with a background job were a finding in all four digests (12, 25, 45 and 41 hits)[^d].
     The prompt text from #425 and #789 didn't remove them.
   - `git checkout -- <path>` refusals came back after #788's habit text: 34 sessions in the Nov 16 digest,
     4 in the window right after it, then 58 in the Nov 23 digest[^d]. The mechanism fix, #1716 (Nov 23: the hook saves the diff, then lets the checkout run), is
     the first one that can stop it. The next retro checks it.
   - `core.commentChar` (#1489, a mechanism) took the `#N:` title loss to zero within two hours[^d].
2. **Briefs state guesses as facts.** This was the largest and steadiest ship-lesson theme: 149 of 437
   lessons with text mention the brief or handoff, and 2 of those were taken[^l]. The process lines show the
   same thing: 21 of 45 mention a brief, handoff or digest[^p]. Examples:
   - #1763's brief guessed about 15 leaking files; CI showed about 60.
   - #1674's brief asked for a $140 prompt eval of a one-line edit.
   - #1425's brief missed an outbox that already existed.

   `kit/skills/briefing/SKILL.md`, part 6, now says a cause, count or size in a brief carries the command
   that showed it, or is labelled a guess.
3. **The screenshot critic gate runs on changes with nothing to show.**
   - The gate (#1504, Nov 21) drew 25 lessons from 25 minions, all of them on Nov 22 to 24[^c].
   - The two text fixes did cut the surprise: 21 lessons before #1701 (Nov 23 4:08 AM), 3 between #1701
     and #1742, and 1 after #1742 (Nov 23 3:14 PM)[^c].
   - What's left is minions routing around the gate. #1773 kept a pointer out of `help-words.js`, #1598 (a code-health run)
     left dead imports in `public/` as "cheaper left to the next minion", and #1719 kept a helper out of
     `model-table.js`[^r].
   - Comment-only, timer-only and title-only edits still need a recorded critic round.
   - Whether the gate should skip these is Cara's call: it reverses part of her 2026-11-21 pick ("Code
     review can be simultaneous, but critics not"). Cara picked "Skip comment-only edits" on 2026-11-24; the gate now leaves a file out when
     every changed line in it is a comment or blank (ADR 0025, amended).
4. **A digest called fixes unbuilt without checking.** The Nov 23 digest called the stop wait and the
   minion search pass "still unbuilt" and named handoff files that don't exist[^h]. Both fixes had already
   shipped: the stop wait in #1031 (Nov 19), which #1717 confirmed, and the minion search pass in #1718
   (Nov 23). This was stale, not lost work. The retro's first draft repeated the digest's claim, and the
   Taskmaster corrected it. Fix, made by the Taskmaster in the process-digest schedule's brief: check
   search_minions and git log before calling a fix unbuilt, and name only handoff paths that exist,
   otherwise say "no handoff".
5. **Shipping got slower and moved off CI.** No fix here, because the Nov 20 to 21 incident minions own it.
   It is recorded so the next retro can compare.
   - Line wait went from a median of 0 s (p90 114 s) in Nov 10 to 16 to 504 s (p90 2763 s) in Nov 17 to 24.
   - The CI run went from a median of 85 s to 213 s.
   - CI-fallback landings went from 8 to 103[^t].
   - Fallbacks per day were 37, 11, 8, 0 and 0 on Nov 20 to 24, so the worst has passed[^t].
   - `src/db.ts` was the top rebase conflict: 1 in week one, 35 in week two[^t].
