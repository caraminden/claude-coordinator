# Creative work (starter)

A scheme's `creative` entry in its review set `extends:` this, sets its own `when:` (`creative: <what
counts here>`, often with a `diff:` for the art folder) and links its taste guide, style notes and
techniques as **Context** guides on the page. The critic reads those guides as the person's
taste; this starter holds only what holds for any creative piece. Without a taste guide the critic
says so and judges on what the brief asks, never on its own taste.

## Blockers

1. **It is what the brief asked for.** A person told only the brief's one line names the piece's
   subject, mood and use from the piece alone. See it: cover the brief, say what it is, then compare.
2. **It follows the taste guide.** Every rule the guide states as a must holds; each broken one is
   one finding, quoting the rule. See it: read the guide's rules first, then look for the breaker.
   A rule the guide doesn't state is a note, never a blocker.
3. **It reads at the size it is used.** A sprite at game size, an icon at 1x, a title in its
   place, a track in its loop: the point of the piece survives. See it: judge the real-size view
   first, then the zoom; a detail that exists only in the zoom doesn't count.
4. **It sits with its neighbours.** Beside the set it joins (the guide's reference pieces, the
   earlier takes a person approved) it looks made by the same hand. See it: put it next to them.
5. **Nothing broken.** No stray pixel, seam, clipped edge, typo, wrong word, click or dropout the
   person would call a mistake. Seen: any piece a person sent back as "off" without a reason.

## Watch-list

- Safe and forgettable: correct on every rule and not one choice a person would remember.
- A flaw copied from a reused part the brief listed as known: a note, not this piece's blocker.
- Detail added to fill space instead of to say something.

## Scale

- 3: correct and usable, you'd forget it by the next piece.
- 4: you'd pick it out of the set; pass.
- 5: the one the person would show someone.

A score under 4 with no blocker is still a fail; the finding is the one change that would add most.

## Calibration board

| Item | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|

A scheme lists three to six pieces a person passed and sent back, with their words, before it
takes the entry off trial (`references/calibrate.md`).

## Known blind spots

- Motion and sound: from frames or notes the critic judges timing on paper, not by ear or eye.
  Name what it couldn't judge instead of passing it.
