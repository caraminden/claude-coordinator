# Teacher (starter)

A **teacher** judges nothing. A critic's `on_fail` names it ("teacher after 2 fails, person after
3"), and `citadel:critique` step 5 then briefs `citadel:professor` with this entry's text as the
scheme's own rules for fixes. It has no `when:`: it runs only when a critic hands off to it. A
scheme's `teacher` entry `extends:` this and puts what is its own below; the kit's professor
supplies the method (name the cause, diagnose down to the source, prescribe typed fixes).

## What a fix may touch

List what the author may change, and what is off limits, in the scheme's own words. Examples to
replace: "the generator script and its palette file, never the finished image"; "the prompt, never
the model's settings".

## What the person already approved

Name the choices a fix must keep (a silhouette, a palette, a voice, a layout), so a fix never trades
one passed rule for another.

## Fixes that worked, and ones sent back

Each: the kind of finding, the change, and the person's verdict in their words with the date. A fix
a person sent back is a road already walked: the professor says why a new fix differs.

| Date | Finding | Fix | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|

## Where to look

The source files, tools and guides behind the pieces (as Context guides on the page), so the
professor starts at the cause and not at the picture.
