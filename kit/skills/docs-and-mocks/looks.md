# A look nobody has decided yet

For a page whose job is to propose a look: a new scheme's, or a screen no theme covers yet. A mock of
a screen that already has a look wears it (`theming.md`), Citadel UI follows `citadel:contraption-ui`,
and the kit's own frame (header, kicker, tags, dock) is Citadel's decided look: none of those is
judged against this file.

## Plan before you build

Write a compact plan, put it in the page's notes or your reply, and check it against the brief:

- **Colour:** 4 to 6 named colours with their values (`Slate #2b3140`: the page;
  `Ember #e2763a`: the one action).
- **Type:** the faces and their roles (display, body, numbers), and the scale.
- **Layout:** one sentence and an ASCII wireframe per take, with how it aligns.
- **What makes it this scheme's:** a line on where the look comes from in the subject itself
  (its world, its materials, its words), not from what pages usually look like.

Then reread the brief. Any part of the plan you would have written for any similar page is a
default, not a choice: change it, and say what you changed and why. Where the brief or a person
names a look, follow it exactly, even when it is one of the tells below.

## The "generated page" tells

Each is fine when the brief asks for it. Unasked, it reads as a default:

- A warm cream page with a heavy serif display and a terracotta or clay accent.
- A near-black page with one acid green or vermilion accent.
- Everything in identical rounded cards, one radius and one soft grey shadow on all of them, with
  gradient washes for decoration.
- A tracked ALL-CAPS label above every heading; one word of a headline picked out in italic or
  colour.
- 01 / 02 / 03 markers on things that aren't a sequence.
- A big number with a small label and a gradient as the opening, by habit.
- A fade-and-slide on every section and a hover lift on every card.

Use them as a checklist when you check the page (step 5), and fix any that crept in.

## Spend boldness in one place

Pick one thing to be memorable (a type treatment, the art, one colour) and keep everything around it
quiet. Before you show the page, take one decoration off.
